Week 1 - Conditionals
/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////
Conditional statements: 
    - Ability to ask questions and answer those questions in order to decide the execution of
      certain code (coding flow).

Symbols to ask questions:
    - ">" : Greater than
    - ">=": Greater than or equal to
    - "<" : Less than
    - "<=": Less than or equal to
    - "==": Equality comparison
    - "!=": Disequality comparison
