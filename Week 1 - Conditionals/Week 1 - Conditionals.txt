Conditional statements: 
    - Ability to ask questions and answer those questions in order to decide the execution of
      certain code (coding flow).

Symbols to ask questions:
    - ">" : Greater than
    - ">=": Greater than or equal to
    - "<" : Less than
    - "<=": Less than or equal to
    - "==": Equality comparison
    - "!=": Disequality comparison

Control flow diagram (read from top to bottom)

elif --> else if: 
    - Allows us to ask a question that takes into account whether or not our previous question 
      had a tre or false answer.
    - Control flow: If we get back a true answer right away in that first if, we are gonna print, 
      then stop (end of the program).
    - Continues to the next instruction if false, if instruction is met, print, then stop(...)
    - "The elif keyword allows you to check multiple expressions for True and execute a block 
      of code as soon as one of the conditions evaluates to True"

else --> "alternative path of execution"
